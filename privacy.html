1up privacy

1up is a golf scorecard for friends. It is built to keep your rounds to yourself.

What 1up collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your scores, players, courses or anything else you enter.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is saved on your own phone: in the app's files on iPhone, or in your browser for the web version. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) deletes it. You can export a copy from Settings at any time.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather. To show the weather for a round, 1up sends the course's town and the round's date and time to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather service.
  • Map lookup. To find that town on the map, the iPhone app asks Apple's map service and the web version asks Open-Meteo's place search. Only the town name is sent.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the text goes wherever you choose to send it, such as Messages.

None of these requests include your name or any other personal information.
